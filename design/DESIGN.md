## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the warmth, sensory nostalgia, and artisanal care of a generational Indian kitchen run by a mother-and-daughter duo. It celebrates traditional culinary craft without feeling dated—bridging cultural authenticity with a vibrant, modern direct-to-consumer digital experience. The interface should feel like receiving a fragrant, hand-packed tin of crisp puris: inviting, grounded, festive, and unmistakably homemade.

The design movement combines **Artisanal Tactile Warmth** with modern editorial structure. Visual pacing is defined by organic curving dividers, subtle kitchen-inspired illustrative stamps, and warm bento grids. The tone avoids slick corporate minimalism; every surface radiates handmade integrity, hospitality, and rich culinary pride.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classic Indian pantry staples and traditional festive celebration:

- **Primary Brand (`#7A1020`):** A deep, celebratory maroon representing heirloom tradition, royal sweetness, and kitchen heritage. Used for high-impact brand elements, key headline text, and solid anchor moments.
- **Secondary Accent (`#F4B72E`):** A radiant turmeric yellow that stimulates appetite, conveys festive optimism, and highlights organic sticker badges, decorative underlines, and flash tags.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#3E7B3F`):** Crisp coriander green, serving as an organic marker for fresh batch drops, vegetarian certification marks, and purity callouts.
- **Utility Action (`#25D366`):** Authentic WhatsApp green reserved exclusively for instant ordering, customer service interactions, and quick-action checkout buttons.
- **Canvas Tones:** The default canvas rests on **Warm Cream (`#FFF6E3`)**, alternating with **Sand (`#F4E2BE`)** for segmented card trays and contrasting sections. Inverted festive footer blocks leverage **Deep Maroon Dark (`#4E0A15`)**.
- **Body & Text Neutral (`#2B1810`):** An aromatic roasted dark brown. Pure pitch blacks or clinical cool greys are strictly prohibited across all surfaces to maintain kitchen warmth.

## Typography

Typography pairs the graceful elegance of **Playfair Display** with the approachable clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Playfair Display introduces warmth, editorial authority, and festive grandeur. Used for hero moments, product collection headings, and sensory storytelling titles.
- **Body & Captions:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides round, welcoming geometry, ensuring high legibility for product descriptions, ingredient lists, and kitchen background details without competing with display elements.
- **Cultural Accents:** Occasional decorative hand-lettered stamps (such as *Maa ke Haath Ka Swad* or *Fresh Batch*) use badge-like rotated styling with uppercase letter-spacing to simulate traditional tin packaging stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a fluid 12-column grid system on desktop (`>1024px`), an 8-column grid on tablet (`768px - 1023px`), and a 4-column layout on mobile (`<768px`).

- **Bento Hierarchy:** Content blocks like "Why Vaaa Foods" and ingredient transparency breakdowns adapt via an organic bento grid where featured mother-daughter kitchen stories occupy expansive spans (`8 cols`), flanked by smaller tactile ingredient badges (`4 cols`).
- **Curved Dividers:** Sections are separated by soft, undulating organic curve transitions (`border-radius: 40px` or SVG wave cuts) rather than rigid horizontal hairline rules.
- **Mobile Sticky Action Anchor:** Mobile interfaces enforce a 72px persistent bottom bar containing a 48px high tap target for direct WhatsApp ordering with safe-area spacing padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth avoids cold, stark industrial drop shadows. Instead, surfaces rely on warm ambient light:

- **Warm Brown Ambient Shadows:** Card elevations use diffused umber shading tinted with `#2B1810` at 6% to 12% opacity (e.g., `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.08)`), evoking daylight falling across an Indian marble countertop.
- **Tonal Layering:** Visual hierarchy is predominantly achieved through alternating surfaces—nesting Sand `#F4E2BE` containers inside Warm Cream `#FFF6E3` canvases.
- **WhatsApp Review Bubbles:** Testimonial speech cards employ a gentle asymmetrical shadow with a slight green tint (`rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.06)`) and a delicate outline to mimic familiar messenger bubbles.
- **Tactile Sticker Offsets:** Badges and stickers use hard-edged, 2px downward micro-offsets with warm golden tones to create a pressed, collectible physical aesthetic.

## Shapes

The shape system blends structured softness with organic kitchen forms:

- **Standard Elements:** Interactive cards, inputs, and standard buttons adhere to Level 2 roundedness (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` on cards).
- **Organic Doodles & Blobs:** Hero imagery, kitchen portraits, and spice highlights are masked within non-uniform organic pill/blob shapes (e.g., `border-radius: 30% 70% 70% 30% / 30% 30% 70% 70%`).
- **Sticker Badges:** Circular and scalloped badge geometry (*"100% Homemade"*, *"No Palm Oil"*) rotate slightly between -3° and +4° to preserve an artisanal, hand-affixed look.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Brand Order):** Solid Deep Maroon (`#7A1020`) with Warm Cream text (`#FFF6E3`). Fully rounded or rounded-lg with comfortable padding (`12px 24px`).
- **Instant WhatsApp CTA:** WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`) featuring a pure white WhatsApp phone/chat glyph. Distinct hover state with an inner warm glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Outlined in Deep Maroon with Sand background fill on hover; never sharp or cold.

### Badges & Chips
- **Sticker Badges:** High-contrast Turmeric Yellow (`#F4B72E`) background with Roasted Brown typography (`#2B1810`). Displayed with subtle angle offsets and dashed or hand-pressed border styling.
- **Dietary & Freshness Chips:** Coriander Green (`#3E7B3F`) background with white text and an organic leaf icon designating fresh batch frying cycles and authentic spice ingredients.

### Cards & Bento Tiles
- **Product Bento Cards:** Warm Cream cards bounded by soft Sand edges (`#F4E2BE`) with generous inner padding. Hover triggers an upward 4px floating translation and warm ambient shadow intensification.
- **WhatsApp Review Cards:** Structured as authentic message bubbles with small tail flourishes, complete with green read-receipt checkmarks, timestamp styling, and customer city locations.

### Form Inputs
- **Inquiry & Order Inputs:** Warm Sand (`#F4E2BE`) background, borderless in rest state, transitioning to a 2px Turmeric Yellow or Deep Maroon outline on focus. Placeholder copy set in muted roasted brown.

### Mobile Sticky Action Bar
- Fixed to viewport bottom with a backdrop blur over Warm Cream (`#FFF6E3/90%`). Features a dual action layout: Price/Pack Selector alongside a full-width WhatsApp CTA button ensuring a minimum 48px touch clearance.
